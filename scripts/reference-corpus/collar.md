# Widget collar

Hold the widget, buy a protective put below spot, and sell a call above it. The
sold call pays for the bought put, so the structure is close to costless at the
price of capping the upside.

Use it when a widget position must be carried through a known event but the
downside is unacceptable. Roll the collar when either leg reaches ten percent of
its original extrinsic value.

The collar is long volatility on the downside and short volatility on the
upside; its risk is not gamma but opportunity cost.
