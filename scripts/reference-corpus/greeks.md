# Greeks

Delta is the rate of change of an option's price with respect to the underlying
price. It is the first derivative and is quoted between minus one and one.

Gamma is the rate of change of delta with respect to the underlying price. It is
the second derivative, is largest at the money, and grows without bound as
expiry approaches.

Implied volatility is the volatility input that makes a pricing model return the
option's traded price. It is a quoted number, not a measurement of the future.
